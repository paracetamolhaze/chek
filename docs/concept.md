# Concept

Started 2026-10-01 (UTC). This file records how the idea was picked, including the name that was dropped.

## Three candidates

| # | Name / ticker | Mascot | Hook | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **RECEIPTS → CHEK** (`$CHEK`) | A thermal-paper receipt with one eyebrow raised | "Receipts or it didn't happen." The project's honesty rules *are* the joke. | **Chosen** |

| 3 | CONE (`$CONE`) | A traffic cone that shows up where it shouldn't | "Under construction. Forever." Build-in-public meme. | Dropped: `$CONE` belongs to BitCone (listed, active community) and dozens of Solana cone tokens. |

## Why the receipt

- **Trust is the product.** A receipt is proof by definition. Every honesty rule we have (no fake partners, no fake audits, no backdating, one CA everywhere) becomes on-brand content instead of fine print.
- **Universal + memeable.** Everyone has a stupid receipt. "Receipts" is already slang for proof. Endless formats: itemized roasts, VOID stamps, CVS-length receipts, Receipt of the Week.
- **A design system that comes from the idea:** thermal paper, dot-matrix type, dashed rules, dot leaders, barcodes, rubber stamps, highlighter. No gradients, no glass, no glowing orbs.
- **A world, not a logo:** Chek (hero), The Shredder (eats evidence), The Coupon (too good to be true), Register #4 (birthplace). Villains = scam archetypes, so lore doubles as anti-scam education.

## Name check (real results, 2026-10-01)

**RECEIPTS / `$RCPT` was dropped** before anything was published:
- an X account "Receipts Coin | $RCPT" (@ReceiptsCoin) already existed;
- a "RECEIPT" token on Robinhood Chain had peaked around $4.7M in Sept 2026 and "RECEIPTS $RCPTS" (generative art) was active — too much confusion, and we don't want to look like a copy.

**CHEK / `$CHEK` — picked:**
- no `$CHEK` on CoinGecko or CoinMarketCap; ~80 dead `$CHEK` launches on pump.fun (none above $50k today);
- `chekcoin` was free on X, Telegram, Vercel and as chekcoin.com / chekcoin.xyz;
- 4 letters → a clickable cashtag on X (cashtags are max 6 letters, `$RECEIPT` would not have been).
- Watch-outs: sounds like Checkmate `$CHECK` (Base, ~$4M) and cheqd `$CHEQ` — always write **$CHEK**. A fintech called Chek Financial exists → no fintech look, no payment claims.

## Why "Chek"

- **Чек** is Russian for receipt.
- **The check** is the bill in English.
- **Check** is what you should do before trusting anything.
- It reads like a meme spelling ("vibe chek", "CA chek") and fits the mascot's one job.

## One-liners

- Tagline: **Receipts or it didn't happen.**
- 10-second version: *$CHEK is a meme coin on Solana with one rule: every claim comes with a receipt.*
- What it isn't: an investment, a utility platform, a promise.

# Lore bible + content plan

## Canon (published)

1. **Register #4** — 03:14 AM, a closed corner store. The register prints a receipt for a purchase nobody made, and keeps printing.
2. **The first line** — the slip tears itself off, reads the internet ("partnership soon", "audit coming", "100x guaranteed"), and prints `CLAIM ....... VOID`.
3. **The Shredder** — eats evidence: deleted tweets, wiped websites, devs who "went for a walk". Leaves confetti. Chek's fear: becoming confetti.
4. **The Coupon** — "100X OFF! TODAY ONLY!" Loud, shiny, always expiring. Chek reads the fine print: "*none of this is real".
5. **Still printing** — every real thing the community does becomes a new line.

## Rules of the world

- Chek can only print what's true. When it tries to print a lie, the ink fades.
- Thermal paper fades over time — Chek's quiet fear. Commits don't fade. That's why everything goes into the build log.
- The Shredder and The Coupon never win in a straight fight; they win when people stop checking.
- The community is "on the register". Real milestones become line items on **The Long Receipt**.

## Next chapters (ideas, not promises)

- 06 **The Long Receipt** — Chek keeps one receipt for everything the community does. It's already longer than Chek.
- 07 **The Coupon's cousin** — the "v2 migration" scam ("swap your tokens here!").
- 08 **Receipt of the Week** winners enter the canon as cameo receipts.
- 09 **The Pen** — the thing that signs. Chek doesn't trust signatures it can't verify.
- Community votes pick chapter themes once the voting tool exists (PLANNED).
